Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 25, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

This website does not use cookies and does not run any analytics or advertising trackers.

Third-Party Services

3. Legal Basis for Processing

4. Sharing Your Information

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We may share data with service providers (e.g., hosting, email transmission) under confidentiality agreements.
  • We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not set cookies and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies of any kind. No consent banner is required, because there is nothing to consent to.

6. Data Retention

To exercise these rights, contact us at samband@spyrja.com.
