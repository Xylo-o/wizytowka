  • We may share data with service providers (e.g., hosting, email transmission) under confidentiality agreements.

5. Cookies and Tracking

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not function properly if cookies are blocked.

6. Data Retention

  • Contact Form Data: retained for up to 2 years to manage correspondence.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@spyrja.is.
